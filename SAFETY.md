# Legal & safety

The app's legal and safety text, word for word (text version 1). It is free software under the
[GNU General Public License, version 3](LICENSE).

## The statements

The app shows these before first use, and asks you to tick each one before it connects to a bike; it asks
again whenever they change. They are also on its Legal & safety screen.

1. I understand that in the EU and many other countries, an e-bike must stop assisting at 25 km/h. Above that, it may legally be a moped that needs approval, registration, insurance, a licence and a helmet.
2. I will use settings above 25 km/h only where it is legal, for example on private land.
3. I understand that higher speeds increase the risk of accidents, injury and damage, and that I am solely responsible.
4. I understand that changing the speed limit may void my warranty and affect my insurance.
5. I understand that this app is unofficial and not affiliated with ENGWE. It is provided “as is”, without warranty or liability (GPL-3.0, sections 15 and 16), and I use it at my own risk.

## Tested on one bike and firmware only

The app shows this once, when you first set up a bike (and again after the text changes), and on its
Legal & safety screen.

Speed changes have been tested only on the ENGWE E26 3.0 Pro with IoT firmware 218CAN. In those tests the limit went back to 25 km/h when the bike was switched off. On other models or firmware, a change might behave differently and could be permanent. Tap 25 when you’re done.

You can check your bike’s IoT firmware version in the ENGWE app.

## Bikes of a model the project hasn't tested

Before the first speed or light change on such a bike, the app asks you to acknowledge this, once per bike
and text version:

> **This model hasn’t been tested yet**
>
> The project hasn’t tested this model. Speed and light changes may not work, or may work differently.
>
> A speed change might not reset when the bike turns off. It could be permanent.
>
> To go back, tap 25.
>
> Reports from riders get bikes verified. Tell us what happens, in a few taps.

Which models are verified, and how a model gets verified: [COMPATIBILITY.md](COMPATIBILITY.md).
