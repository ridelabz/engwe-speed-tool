# Support

This project is **community-supported: there is no individual support.** Nobody is paid to answer, and
the maintainers can't help with a particular bike, order, warranty or the ENGWE app.

- **Questions:** ask in Discussions, where other riders can help.
- **Something went wrong in the app:** in the app, choose **Report** in the menu, then **Something went
  wrong**: it shows you every line of the session log, then saves it in Downloads and fills in the form
  for you to attach it. Or open an issue with the form **Something went wrong** and attach a session log
  (Diagnostics → tap the log → Save to Downloads).
- **How the app worked on your bike:** in the app, choose **Report** in the menu, then **How it worked on my
  bike**, or open an issue with that form. Reports like this get models verified; see
  [COMPATIBILITY.md](COMPATIBILITY.md).
- **Safety and the law:** read [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md) first.

Never post a Bluetooth address, a serial number, photos of the bike's frame number or screenshots of the
ENGWE app.
